The paper investigates the quality and gender representation of machine translation datasets for three Ethiopian languages: Afan Oromo, Amharic, and Tigrinya. Using automated analyses and human evaluation. It emphasizes that collecting more data does not necessarily improve quality and recommends more careful dataset curation, quality control, and early evaluation of gender bias to support fairer and more reliable language technologies for low-resource African languages.</i>'
